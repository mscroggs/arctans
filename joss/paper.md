# Summary

Machin's formula is

$$\frac{\uppi}{4}=4\arctan\left(\frac{1}{5}\right)-\arctan\left(\frac{1}{239}\right),$$

or, equivalently,

$$\uppi=16\arctan\left(\frac{1}{5}\right)-4\arctan\left(\frac{1}{239}\right).$$
